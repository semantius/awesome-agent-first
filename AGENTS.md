# AGENTS.md

Instructions for AI agents working in this repository.

## Verify everything you write down

- Never present unverified information as fact. Every claim you put in this repository or report to the maintainer needs a current source: the vendor's own site, documentation, repository, API reference or a live response.
- Training knowledge is not a source. It is undated and may be wrong or out of date. Use it only to decide where to look, never as the thing you cite.
- If you could not verify something, say so plainly instead of filling the gap. Leave it out, or mark it as unverified and say what would settle it.
- When you report findings, say what you checked and where, so the maintainer can tell verified claims from unverified ones.

## Entry descriptions

- A description says what the product is, in neutral wording drawn from the vendor's own description of itself: homepage title or meta description, repository description, or documentation. Rewrite taglines and marketing claims rather than copying them.
- Keep descriptions at the level of the product. How an entry meets clauses 1 to 7 belongs in the assessment and the pull request body, not in the description.
- Interfaces, licence and hosting go in the tags, so don't repeat them in the description.
- Follow the entry format in [contributing.md](contributing.md): one line, starting with a capital and ending with a period, with no counts.

## Assessing candidates

- Assess against the clauses, quality bar and exclusions in [contributing.md](contributing.md), and cite the vendor material behind each finding.
- Before suggesting an entry, check that it is not already in [README.md](README.md), [considered.md](considered.md) or [agent-only.md](agent-only.md).

## Workflow

- The maintainer works on `main`. Do not create branches. The branch rule in contributing.md is for outside contributors.
- Commit only when asked.
